How to Use
📖
Nakshatra Speaks Guide
Everything you need to know to unlock your cosmic blueprint through Chaldean Numerology.
⚡ Getting Started
1
Open the Calculator
Tap the Calculator icon in the bottom navigation bar. This is where all your numerology readings begin.
2
Choose a Reading Type
Select from 5 tabs — Name, Mobile, Vehicle, Life Path or Business — depending on what you want to analyse.
3
Enter Your Details
Type your name, number, or date of birth in the input field. The app calculates your Chaldean vibration in real time as you type.
4
Tap Calculate & Read
Press the ✦ Check Results ✦ button. Your full numerology reading with personality traits, career insights, remedies and more will appear instantly.
🔢 Calculator Types
Name Numerology
Enter your full name as used officially. The Chaldean system converts each letter to a number and sums the vibration to reveal your personality, lucky numbers, and destiny path.
Mobile Number
Enter your 10-digit mobile number. The app reduces all digits to a root number and tells you if your phone number supports or hinders your personal vibration.
Vehicle Number
Enter your vehicle registration number (e.g. AP16CX1234). Numbers in the plate are summed to find the vibrational energy your vehicle carries into your life.
Life Path Number
Enter your date of birth. Your Life Path is the most important number in your chart — it reveals your life's purpose, natural talents, and the challenges you're meant to overcome.
Business Name
Enter your brand or business name. Find out if it carries a vibration that attracts wealth, success, and positive partnerships — or if a spelling change could improve its energy.
✦ Reading Your Results
Root Number & Planet
The large number shown is your root (single-digit) number, and below it is your ruling planet. This is the core vibration of whatever you calculated.
Compound Number
The two-digit number before reduction (e.g. 23) is your compound number. Tap the root number grid to explore the deep meaning of all compound numbers for that planet.
Luck Score & Vibration Bar
The coloured bar shows whether the vibration is favourable, moderate, or challenging. Higher scores mean the number works strongly in your favour.
Reading Tabs
Scroll through five insight categories: Personal, Career, Love, Health, and Remedies. Each tab gives tailored guidance based on your number's energy.
Save to Reports
Tap Save to Reports to store any reading permanently. Access all saved readings anytime from the Reports tab in the bottom bar.

💡 Pro Tips
✨
Use your full birth name
For the most accurate Name reading, enter the name exactly as it appears on your birth certificate — not a nickname or shortened form.
🔢
Try spelling variations
Small spelling changes can shift your number's vibration significantly. Use the Name calculator to test different spellings of your name or business.
📅
Check Daily Forecast every morning
The universal day number changes every day. Opening the app first thing helps you align your actions with the day's cosmic energy.
💾
Save important readings
Always save a reading after you calculate it. Saved reports let you compare numbers across names, dates, or numbers side by side.
🌱
Apply your remedies consistently
Remedies like gemstones, fasting days, and mantras work best when followed regularly. Check the Remedies tab after every reading for personalised guidance.
